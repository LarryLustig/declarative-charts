# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind to all people, at all times. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code,
not people.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space belonging to this project.

## Expected

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you think someone is wrong.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a request. This is a personal project
  with a stated scope; "no" is not hostility.
- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This is broken" is less useful than
  markup that reproduces the problem.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report anything that concerns you to larrylustig@gmail.com. Reports are handled
privately.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, and may block accounts, for
behaviour judged inappropriate. There is no committee and no appeals process —
this is one person maintaining a library, and the aim is simply that
participating here is not unpleasant.
