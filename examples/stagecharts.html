Stage Chart Examples

Visualize pipelines and workflows with variable-sized stages

Basic Stage Charts

Stage charts show connected shapes where each stage's area is proportional to its value. Unlike funnel charts, values can go up and down:

Project Pipeline (Values Vary)

<dc-stage-chart width="500" height="450" stage-size="value">
  <dc-title>Project Pipeline</dc-title>
  <dc-stage value="50" label="Backlog"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="120" label="In Progress"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="80" label="Review"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="200" label="Done"></dc-stage>
</dc-stage-chart>

Small Values and Zero Handling

Stage charts automatically handle small and zero values. Labels that don't fit inside small shapes are suppressed and shown via popup on hover instead.

Auto-Fit Label Suppression

When stage-size="value" and a stage has a very small value relative to others, its shape may be too small for labels. The chart automatically suppresses labels that don't fit and enables hover popups:

Mixed Large and Small Values

<dc-stage-chart width="500" height="450"
                stage-size="value"
                connector="arrow">
  <dc-stage value="1000" label="Major"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="50" label="Medium"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="5" label="Small"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="800" label="Large"></dc-stage>
</dc-stage-chart>

Hover over the small stage to see its label in a popup.

Extreme Value Differences

<dc-stage-chart width="500" height="450"
                stage-size="value"
                shape="circle"
                connector="arrow">
  <dc-stage value="500" label="Leads"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="100" label="Qualified"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="10" label="Proposals"></dc-stage>
  <dc-stage value="2" label="Closed Won"></dc-stage>
</dc-stage-chart>

Small circles automatically show popups on hover.
